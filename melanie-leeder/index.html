In a time and society where being reserved was considered polite, is it possible to keep secrets without consequences?

1937. It’s the night of Fiona Beaumont’s 21st birthday party at the Savoy Ballroom, London, and she has it all: wealth, beauty, and effortless elegance. Then the world plunges into war.

2007. Hazel Stevens, her housemaid for nearly 20 years, knows she must tell her story to her daughter Mary before it’s too late.

Two women from starkly different backgrounds would never have imagined how their lives would entwine so intricately.

About Melanie

Melanie Leeder writes historical fiction. She lives on the coast with her husband, three children and two cats, and her family has moved around the country and overseas.

With a degree in Speech and Language Therapy, she has always been fascinated by psychology and human behaviour, and mixes it with her love of history, art and architecture. Reticence is her first novel.
