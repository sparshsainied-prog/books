The Snake Lady

Nancy is a mother of three boys in New England, and she has been an outdoors woman and nature lover her whole life.

She started her own environmental education program on native New England snakes, and has given talks to every age group about the secret jobs snakes do in an ecosystem.

She has retired from rescuing and raising snakes, but plenty of people still call her The Snake Lady. These days she shares her love of snakes through her picture books.

Leroy the chameleon is, in Nancy’s words, still the funniest reptile in my collection.

Sassy has found readers from Connecticut all the way to California and New Mexico.
