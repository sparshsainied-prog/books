Valentina’s Fun Day Out

Nanny and grandad take Valentina to Crystal Peaks shopping centre, to a children’s shop called Dog and Gate.

Dog and Gate is every child’s dream shop, because you can shop with your dog. There is also a big ice cream parlour in the middle of the shop, which makes the shopping even more magical.

Valentina has an amazing time shopping and making new friends.

Belinda Booth

Belinda was born in Bulawayo, Zimbabwe. As a teenager she presented the children’s TV show Teen Scene, and she wrote her first book at 21, while living in Botswana. After time in France and London, she moved to Sheffield.

The idea for Valentina came from a real trip to Crystal Peaks with her husband John and their granddaughter. Every character in the book is a real person, and there’s a nod to Sheffield’s Supertram in there too.

She’s also a poet, writes for the online magazine Fusion, and has finished What Georgie Did, a book for teenagers about the environment and British culture.
