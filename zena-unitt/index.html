Even from the afterlife, a mother knows best.

Kandy is dead. But death isn’t the end.

She’s granted ten post-passing privileges. Ten chances to revisit the world she left behind. She can’t resist meddling in her daughter Eloise’s love life. Especially when Eloise falls for a man Kandy knows is all wrong.

But time is running out. And she has another promise to keep. Her best friend Almira has spent years searching for the daughter stolen from her. Kandy may be the only one who can help.

A tender and mischievous tale of love, loss and the unbreakable bonds that defy even death.
