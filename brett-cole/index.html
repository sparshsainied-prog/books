Brett wrote it over some two decades, coming back to it at different times as a kind of journal.

Where the title comes from

The pink bubble is a meditation from Shakti Gawain’s Creative Visualisation, one where “affirmations are made while visualisations are being created”.

Brett adapted the book’s title from it.

Anton’s shelf

Anton is based on the narrator of Sartre’s Nausea. Brett also names Crime and Punishment and The Catcher in the Rye as influences, for the way their characters work through inner turmoil.

About Brett

Brett Cole was born in Nambour, Queensland, and lives in the south west of Western Australia.

He was a printer, then took a Bachelor of Arts in English and Comparative Literature and Philosophy and became a teacher. He now works as a disability support worker. The Pink Bubble is his first book, and he was one of the authors at the 2025 Indie Author Book Fair in Bunbury.
