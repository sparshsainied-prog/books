Waiting for the Sun, by Adelle Woods

Waiting for the Sun

Between the bullying at school and her unloving father at home, Lacey has forgotten what true safety feels like.

Then she meets kind and protective Alex, and parts of her world seem slightly brighter. As they grow closer, her scepticism about friendship lingers, making it harder for her to trust than she anticipated.

Young adult. Typewriter Pub, January 2024. 175 pages in paperback.

Paperback on Amazon Kindle edition Goodreads

Adelle Woods

Adelle is a young adult contemporary writer from Adelaide, South Australia. Waiting for the Sun is her first novel.

When she’s not dreaming up stories, she’s reading copious amounts of books or writing poetry. She writes poems about love, hope and Jesus, and posts them as Adelle Rogers on @poetrybyadelle.

In October 2026 she shared the book with readers at the Adelaide Indie Book Festival.
