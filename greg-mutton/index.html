Delos 5

Delos 5 has an orbit that has baffled human astro-physicists for over a century. That, and the planet’s intense geo-magnetic structure, have made it one of the richest sources of Barainium in the galaxy.

The Guardians, a division of the Earth Coalition Space Corps, transport all Barainium ore on Delos 5. But fierce new competition for the mineral causes huge problems for Galactic Mining, the company that holds a thousand-year mining lease. As profits are squeezed, mines close and workers are discarded.

Banding together, the Outcasts begin a guerrilla campaign to force Galactic to honour its agreements. Now the Guardians must keep moving the ore while civil war threatens the planet.

Science fiction. Paperback and ebook, September 2026. 330 pages in paperback.

Big Bertha

Captain Howard Mosley is a decorated officer of the Earth Coalition Space Corps, now second in command of the Guardian Battalion on Delos 5. He also commands Big Bertha, the largest road train on the planet.

He has a secret. As a young man he played on his mother’s recordings, and one of them, Moonlight Heartbreak, became the biggest blues hit in history, mostly because of his haunting guitar riffs. Virtually nobody in his military life knows who he really is.

Greg Mutton

Greg lives in a quiet bayside village on the mid north coast of New South Wales, and writes sci-fi adventure. His stories are about people who rise to the challenge, all with a distinct Aussie flavour.

He brings long and varied experience as an engineer to his books. In March 2023 he launched Incident at Zyralin 4 in the Dolphin Room at the Tea Gardens Hotel.

“I write about real characters, putting them into extraordinary circumstances and seeing how they respond.”Greg, at the Zyralin 4 launch (News Of The Area)
